💡 How to Improve Your Score

🔒 What is HTTPS?

HTTPS encrypts the data sent between your website and your visitors. Without it, hackers on the same network (like public Wi-Fi) can steal passwords, credit cards, and session cookies.

Fix: Obtain a free SSL certificate from Let's Encrypt or your hosting provider.

🛡️ What is HSTS?

Even with HTTPS, a user might accidentally type http://. HSTS (HTTP Strict Transport Security) forces the browser to always use the secure HTTPS version, preventing "downgrade attacks."

Fix: Add this header to your web server:
Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains

🖱️ What is Clickjacking?

Clickjacking is a sneaky attack where hackers hide your website inside an invisible frame (iframe) on their own malicious site. Users might think they are clicking a button on the hacker's page, but they are actually clicking a hidden button on your site.

Fix: Add this header to your web server:
X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN

🔍 What is a Meta Description?

This is the short text snippet that appears under your link in Google search results. While it doesn't directly affect ranking, a good description increases the likelihood of users clicking your link.

Fix: Add this tag inside your HTML <head>:
<meta name="description" content="A brief summary of your site...">

⚡ Boost Speed

Compress large images, use a CDN (like Cloudflare), and minify your CSS/JS files.
